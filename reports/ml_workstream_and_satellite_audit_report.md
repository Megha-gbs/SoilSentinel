## 3. Epistemic Assessment of Target Labels

1. **Heuristic Risk Engine Labels:**
   - The original composite risk score in `feature_table.csv` is generated by deterministic mathematical thresholds:
     $$\text{Risk Score} = 0.30 \cdot \text{MoistureDeficit} + 0.25 \cdot \text{PrecipDeficit} + 0.15 \cdot \text{ThermalStress} + 0.15 \cdot \text{SOC\_Depletion} + 0.15 \cdot \text{pH\_Deviation}$$
   - Agreement between a machine learning classifier and this target proves high approximation fidelity to the rule formula; it **does not** prove independent empirical detection of physical soil degradation.
2. **Laboratory-Measured Ground-Truth Targets (WoSIS & Soil Surveys):**
   - Soil Organic Carbon ($g C / kg$) measured via Walkley-Black wet oxidation or dry combustion.
   - Soil pH in $1:1$ or $1:2.5$ aqueous solution (`ph_h2o`).
   - Electrical Conductivity ($EC$ in $dS/m$, $1:2.5$ extract or saturation extract) measuring soil salinity.
   - Exchangeable Sodium Percentage ($ESP\%$) and Cation Exchange Capacity ($CEC$).
3. **Independent Degradation Classifications Grounded in Analytical Soil Science:**
   - **Saline Soil Degradation:** $EC > 2.0\text{ dS/m}$ ($1:2.5$ extract) and $pH < 8.5$.
   - **Sodic / Alkaline Soil Degradation:** $pH > 8.5$ (dispersion of clay colloids, severe hydraulic conductivity collapse).
   - **Acidic Soil Degradation:** $pH < 5.5$ (aluminum toxicity and micronutrient fixation).
   - **Healthy / Normal Soil:** $5.5 \le pH \le 8.5$ and $EC \le 2.0\text{ dS/m}$.
