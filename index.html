01THE GROUND BENEATH US

We look at the harvest.
We should look below.

Soil is not just the ground we stand on. It is a living, changing system—holding water, cycling nutrients, and supporting the food we depend on.

Yet many of the signals that tell us soil is under stress are difficult to see. SoilSentinel brings available soil and climate evidence into one clearer picture, helping people ask better questions and take informed next steps.
